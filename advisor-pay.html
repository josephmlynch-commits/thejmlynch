How financial advisors get paid

Knowing how someone is paid tells you what to ask them.

"Financial advisor" isn't one job. The same title can describe a salesperson, a fiduciary planner, or an educator, and how each one is paid shapes the advice you get. Here's how the four most common models compare.

The one that trips people up

Fee-based and fee-only are not the same. A fee-based advisor charges advisory fees but can still earn commissions on products. A fee-only advisor is paid only by the client and never earns commissions. The names sound alike, so it's worth asking directly.

Three questions to ask any advisor

  1. How are you paid, and does that change based on what I buy?
  2. Are you a fiduciary for everything you recommend to me, all the time?
  3. Where can I verify your registration? (Look them up for free on FINRA BrokerCheck.)
